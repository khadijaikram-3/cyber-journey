# Incident Response (Insights from a 30-year Security Veteran)

## The Golden Rule: Keep Your Cool
During a data breach, everyone around you will be panicking. As a security professional, you must be the "cool guy in the room." Have the pause in the conversation. Be the person who can confidently say, "I know what's going on."

## The 2-Step Priority
1. **Contain the Breach:** Stop the data loss immediately. This is your #1 priority. If it means shutting down a server, a data center, or communications, do it. Stop the hemorrhaging of data.
2. **Investigate the Breach:** Once the bleeding is stopped, figure out what happened.

## Real-World Example
An engineer for a financial company bought a server off eBay. When they fired it up, it hadn't been wiped. It contained 20 million credit card records. 
- **The Lesson:** This triggered a massive review of third-party data centers. Companies must have strict controls for how third parties wipe servers they no longer use.
