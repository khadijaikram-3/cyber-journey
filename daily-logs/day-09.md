## 1. Theory (Google Cybersecurity)
- **Focus:** Security Frameworks, Security Controls, Compliance, and the CIA Triad.
- **Key Learnings:**
  - **Frameworks** are guidelines (plans) to mitigate risks. **Controls** are specific safeguards (e.g., mandatory training).
  - **The 4 Core Components of a Framework:** 1. Identify goals (e.g., GDPR compliance). 2. Set guidelines. 3. Implement processes. 4. Monitor and communicate results.
  - **NIST:** U.S. agency providing voluntary frameworks (NIST CSF, NIST RMF).
  - **FERC-NERC:** For the power grid. Mandatory reporting of incidents.
  - **FedRAMP:** Standardizes cloud security for the U.S. government.
  - **CIS:** Provides actionable controls to safeguard systems.
  - **GDPR:** Protects E.U. residents. Requires breach notification within **72 hours**.
  - **PCI DSS:** Protects credit card data to reduce fraud.
  - **HIPAA:** Protects patient health information (PHI) in the U.S. Governed by Privacy, Security, and Breach Notification rules. HITRUST helps meet HIPAA compliance.
  - **ISO:** International standards for technology and management.
  - **SOC 1 & SOC 2:** Created by AICPA. Assesses user access policies at different organizational levels to ensure financial compliance and data safety.
