# Day 7 - The History of Cyber Attacks & Malware

### November 20, 2026

## 1. Theory (Google Cybersecurity)
- **Focus:** History of cyber attacks, malware types, social engineering, and incident response.
- **Key Historical Attacks:**
  - **Brain Virus (1986):** Created by Alvi brothers to track pirated medical software. Accidentally spread globally via infected floppy disks, slowing down business productivity.
  - **Morris Worm (1988):** Created by Robert Morris to measure the size of the internet. Failed to track infected computers, causing them to crash from memory overload. Led to the creation of CERTs (Computer Emergency Response Teams).
  - **LoveLetter (2000):** The first major social engineering attack. Tricked users into opening an "I Love You" attachment, which emailed itself to their entire address book. Caused $10 billion in damages.
  - **Equifax Breach (2017):** Data breach caused by failure to patch known vulnerabilities. 143 million records stolen (PII). Resulted in a $575 million settlement.
- **Malware Types:** Virus (requires user action), Worm (self-replicates), Ransomware (encrypts data for payment), Spyware (gathers data secretly).
- **Social Engineering:** Manipulating human error to gain access. Relies on Authority, Intimidation, Consensus, Scarcity, Familiarity, Trust, and Urgency.
- **Incident Response:** The #1 priority is to **contain the breach** (stop data loss) before investigating.
